About Me

My name is Jonathan Hopkins and I am a Cybersecurity senior at the University of North Carolina Wilmington. I am currently building this cybersecurity awareness site for my MIS 310 class.

I have a strong interest in cybersecurity. Currently, I have an internship with Carolina Cyber Network where I work in the Security Operations Center. That experience is one of the reasons I decided that I wanted to build a site that focuses on growing cybersecurity threat awareness.

My goal with this site is to help make basic cybersecurity habits easier for people to understand, things like spotting suspicious emails, using strong passwords, and simply thinking before you click.
